# Content-based style selection

[中文](style-selection.md)

Identify the task: approve a decision, understand evidence, learn a product, feel a brand, learn a method or assess a design. Consider audience expertise, formality, evidence, density, brand constraints and image quality. A technology company's board review may still need an executive direction rather than a dark technology treatment.

Prioritize explicit templates and branding, the user's aesthetic request, content/audience fit, then the suggestions below. Compare two plausible directions internally with concrete evidence and drawbacks, select one, and state the reason briefly. Do not fabricate numeric style scores or require a questionnaire by default.

| Direction | Fit | Information and composition | Example palette | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive | Management, budgets, approvals | Conclusion with evidence; native charts and action tables; stable grid | White `#FFFFFF`, navy `#183247`, blue `#2864B4` | None/fade; continuity for related evidence |
| Research | Analysis, experiments, methods | Charts/tables dominate; direct labels and adjacent caveats | Gray `#F6F7F8`, charcoal `#20252B`, teal `#247A78` | Mostly static; reveal useful evidence layers |
| Product/technology | Products, systems, implementation | Flows, architecture, interface evidence; active path indicated by color | Deep blue `#121E2A`, white `#EAF0F5`, cyan `#70D3D7` | Object continuity, restrained pan/zoom or Morph |
| Editorial/brand | Culture, community, brand stories | Purposeful photos with asymmetric text/image balance; optional serif titles | Paper `#F5F1E8`, brown `#362E2B`, terracotta `#A85C46` | Image continuity/fade; annular only when justified |
| Teaching | Training and explanation | Concepts, examples, comparisons and practice; readable labels | Blue-gray `#EEF4F7`, blue `#243D52`, orange `#A65322` | Progress with the explanation |
| Architecture/portfolio | Space, design review, portfolios | Photos/plans, proportions and annotations; preserve evidence | Stone `#E9E6DF`, black `#252725`, brown `#80705D` | Related views without distorting scale |

These are extensible directions, not a template catalog. Verify font availability. Executive, research and technical copy usually benefits from clear sans-serif hierarchy; editorial/portfolio titles may use a serif. Teaching prioritizes readability.

Style controls overall voice, fonts and colors. Layout follows each page's evidence. Motion explains relations between states. None requires the same layout on every slide. Preserve a main direction in a normal deck, borrowing a useful method such as direct chart labels without changing the visual identity. A grouped style sampler is a separate use case.

If pictures are weak but data is strong, choose an evidence-led direction. If learning steps matter more than atmosphere, prioritize teaching or product explanation. For formal approval, increase explicit evidence and conditions while reducing decorative movement. If none fits, create a direction from the material rather than distorting it to match a preset.

Review whether composition and evidence support the stated rationale. Multi-style samples should differ in information organization as well as color. Reusing the last wheel, photograph or palette is not a selection method.
