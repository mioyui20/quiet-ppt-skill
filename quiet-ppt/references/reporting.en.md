# Density and speaker notes

[中文](reporting.md)

Default to a speaker who needs support to present without substantial additional writing. Adjust to an explicitly requested sparse keynote or reading deck without forcing a mode picker.

| Mode | On-slide content | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready to present, default | Title, explained points, evidence/action | Natural talk track for each content slide |
| Explicit sparse keynote | One idea and a strong visual focus | More explanation in notes |
| Independent reading | Background, definitions, sources, limitations and reasoning | Main content stands alone |

A Chinese text-led content slide can start around 140–240 characters; an English slide around 70–120 words. Evidence replaces some prose. Notes may start at 180–320 Chinese characters or 110–170 English words, adjusted to duration. These are flexible starting points, not quotas.

Each point explains the relevant subject, reason, example or action. A proposal needs arrangements and conditions; progress needs evidence and unresolved work; analysis needs findings and interpretation; decisions need options, tradeoffs and action. Do not force every structure into every slide or invent results to make a paragraph longer.

Write complete, speakable short paragraphs, not “explain these bullets.” Explain evidence, implications and conditions, then connect to the next page. Keep important conclusions on the slide too, separate sources from the script, and avoid unsupported facts.

## Revision example

For a fictional bookstore, “natural light and quiet seats” is too thin to explain how the space will work. A stronger page describes:

- Reading seats: window tables and individual seats; determine quantity from dimensions and circulation needs.
- Sound zoning: separate discussion from quiet reading; explain activity times/boundaries.
- Circulation: display the theme list at the entrance and keep the main route clear.
- Feedback: observe where visitors stay before changing seating/displays.

Notes connect these choices: seating serves different habits, separation reduces conflicting uses, the list helps discovery, and observations test the concept. Identify this as a proposal, not a business result.

Review using only the page and notes. The speaker should explain the essential reasoning without researching a missing link. Add supported explanation when thin; keep genuinely short points short.
