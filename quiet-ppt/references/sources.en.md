Concrete feedback shaped the workflow: an initial sample was too sparse for a speaker to use without preparation, and an annular demo could be mistaken for a mandatory template. The resulting defaults are explained content with talk tracks, content/audience-based style selection, and independent motion selection.

Reviewed references include [Anthropic's PPTX skill](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/pptx/SKILL.md) and [frontend-slides](https://github.com/zarazhangrui/frontend-slides/blob/main/SKILL.md). Shared good practices include editable evidence, visual/package review and concrete previews. This project's instructions, helper and layouts were independently written. External code, templates and instruction text are not included. The six directions are recommendations, not an exhaustive taxonomy.

The excerpt of [this Bilibili video](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1tHs6e7Eqm/) informed visual continuity only. It is not redistributed and does not establish the original deck's implementation. Microsoft documentation informed Morph matching and OOXML; see motion.en.md.
